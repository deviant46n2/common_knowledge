# Open Humans — Project Profile

- **Category:** Participant-controlled personal data, self-research, and community research
- **Snapshot:** November 2026
- **Status:** Initial profile; privacy and governance review remains incomplete
- **Primary site:** https://www.openhumans.org/

## What it is

Open Humans is a program of the Open Humans Foundation, a US nonprofit. It supports individuals and communities in accessing, exploring, and selectively sharing personal data for education, self-research, health-related research, and citizen science.

Its platform documentation describes participant accounts, data imports, browser-based analysis notebooks, community activities, and researcher-created projects. Members can choose which activities or researchers receive access to selected data. Participants can also create and share analysis tools and community activities.

## What role it fills

Open Humans provides an example of research participation organized around people and their own data, rather than only around institutional data repositories or researcher-controlled study databases. It also creates a space for self-research and community-generated research questions.

## Strengths and potentially relevant design choices

- Makes individual participation and data sharing an explicit choice.
- Provides ways for participants to explore their own data and receive data back from research activities.
- Supports community projects and tools, not only researcher-led recruitment.
- The organization describes community-elected board representation and open-source software components.
- Its public data and activity guidelines explicitly discuss identifiability, data minimization, participant exit, and responsible use.

## Limitations and risks

- Personal data can remain identifiable even when names are removed or pseudonyms are used; genetic, health, location, and behavioral data can be especially sensitive.
- Consent to share data with a project does not remove the researcher's responsibility for security, ethics, and lawful use.
- Open Humans' own terms state that it does not independently verify the accuracy or legality of user-posted data.
- Data withdrawal and deletion across downstream copies and publications can be difficult; participants need clear explanations before sharing.
- The profile does not independently audit the platform's security, financial sustainability, or effectiveness of governance.

## Relevance to Common Knowledge

Open Humans is important because a commons cannot treat people merely as data sources. Participants need agency over contribution, meaningful consent, understandable data practices, and a way to benefit from or inspect research built with their contributions. Common Knowledge should distinguish open research outputs from personal data that should remain under controlled access.

## Assessment

**Preliminary assessment:** A valuable case for participant agency and personal-data stewardship. It also demonstrates why “open science” must not mean indiscriminate publication of sensitive human data.
