# Public Lab — Project Profile

- **Category:** Community science, environmental monitoring, open hardware, and grassroots research
- **Snapshot:** November 2026
- **Status:** Initial profile; current organizational activity and platform state need direct verification
- **Primary site:** https://publiclab.org/

## What it is

Public Lab is a nonprofit and community network associated with community-led environmental investigation. Its history is rooted in grassroots mapping and community responses to environmental harm, including the Deepwater Horizon oil spill. The community has developed and shared low-cost tools, methods, maps, research notes, and practical knowledge for environmental monitoring.

Public Lab's archived website and external community-science descriptions emphasize peer learning, open tools, DIY methods, and enabling communities to investigate issues that directly affect them.

## What role it fills

Public Lab represents a form of research participation in which affected communities can help define questions, develop instruments, collect observations, interpret findings, and use evidence in local decision-making. This is materially different from a model where the public is invited only to classify data generated by professional researchers.

## Strengths and potentially relevant design choices

- Centers community questions and local knowledge.
- Connects practical tools, methods, documentation, and peer support.
- Emphasizes low-cost and open hardware approaches that can reduce barriers to participation.
- Supports research and knowledge production in contexts where communities may have limited access to institutional science.
- Demonstrates that research infrastructure includes social networks, education, and practical capacity—not only software.

## Limitations and open questions

- Current platform status, community activity, staffing, and organizational finances require direct verification; the public site currently identifies itself as a static read-only archive.
- Community-generated measurements may require calibration, validation, and careful communication before being compared with regulatory or laboratory measurements.
- Environmental monitoring can create safety, privacy, legal, and political risks for participants.
- The profile does not yet include interviews with community members or an independent evaluation of research outcomes.

## Relevance to Common Knowledge

Public Lab is a reminder that Common Knowledge should not define research solely through academic publishing or computational infrastructure. A credible commons must support people who are closest to a problem, respect their priorities, and help connect community evidence with appropriate scientific expertise without taking control away from those communities.
