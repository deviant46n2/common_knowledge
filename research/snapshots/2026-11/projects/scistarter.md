# Project Profile: SciStarter

- **Snapshot:** 2026-11
- **Profile last verified:** 2026-11-26
- **Official website:** https://scistarter.org/
- **Status:** Active citizen-science discovery and participation service
- **Confidence:** Medium; this is an initial profile based on public-facing service descriptions

## Overview

SciStarter is a service that helps people discover citizen-science opportunities and connect with projects. Its public-facing tools allow people to search for projects by keyword, topic, and other filters. It also provides introductory resources and training for people who want to participate.

## Purpose and users

- **Intended problem:** Help members of the public find research projects that match their interests and opportunities to contribute.
- **Users:** Prospective citizen scientists, project organizers, and researchers seeking participants.
- **Main use cases:** Project discovery, volunteer recruitment, introductory training, and connection to citizen-science opportunities.

## What it provides

The current public website describes:
- Search and discovery of projects by keyword and topic.
- Filters and project information to help people identify relevant opportunities.
- Accounts for tracking participation and receiving recommendations.
- Introductory citizen-science training resources.

SciStarter also points users toward projects and platforms operated by other organizations.

## Technical design

The platform's underlying architecture, integrations, data model, and APIs were not assessed in this initial profile.

## Maturity and evidence of operation

SciStarter is an established public-facing discovery service referenced by citizen-science resources and project catalogs. This profile does not independently assess the completeness or freshness of its directory, project coverage by region or language, or the effectiveness of its recruitment and retention tools.

## Openness

Source licensing, data licensing, exportability, and API availability were not verified in this profile.

## Governance and economics

Institutional structure, funding, governance, and contributor rights were not investigated in this initial pass.

## Strengths

- Makes it easier to discover participation opportunities across a range of topics.
- Offers a discovery layer that can connect people to projects hosted elsewhere.
- Provides introductory training and resources for prospective participants.

## Limitations and risks

- A directory or discovery service does not itself provide the research workflow, data stewardship, analysis, or publication infrastructure for each listed project.
- Directory coverage, currency, geographic balance, language support, and accessibility need evaluation.
- The service's governance, funding, and data practices remain unverified.

## Relevance to Common Knowledge

SciStarter illustrates a potentially important but narrower layer of the research ecosystem: helping people find where they can participate. Common Knowledge should investigate whether discovery and onboarding are persistent barriers, and how project catalogs could interoperate with research records and contributor recognition.

## Assessment

**Documented fact:** SciStarter publicly offers a searchable citizen-science project discovery service and introductory participation resources.

**Assessment:** Discovery and participation infrastructure should be analyzed separately from platforms that host and manage research tasks. The two can complement one another.

**Open questions:** How comprehensive and current is the directory? How are projects selected and maintained? What user data is collected? How are projects and contributors represented across external platforms? What is the organization's governance and funding model?
