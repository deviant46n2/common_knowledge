## Central question

What infrastructure lets independent participants share research services, data, and computing without placing all control in one operator?

## Scope

Map volunteer and distributed computing, grid/edge systems, federation, peer-to-peer coordination, distributed storage, mirrors, identity, scheduling, verification, security, resilience, cost, energy, and operational burden. Distinguish distribution of machines from distribution of ownership and power.

## Questions to investigate

- How are participants coordinated and trusted?
- How are tasks verified and abuse handled?
- What fails when nodes disappear or behave maliciously?
- Which workloads benefit from distribution?
- What are the real costs and environmental impacts?
- Who controls identity, data, and governance?

## Expected outputs

- Architecture landscape
- project profiles
- comparison of trust and coordination models
- deployment and operational analysis
- candidate integrations and bounded experiments.
