## Central question

What makes research inspectable, reproducible, verifiable, correctable, and trustworthy across people, tools, institutions, and time?

## Scope

Investigate research software and computational environments, data/code availability, metadata, provenance, versioning, preregistration, replication, validation, peer review, corrections, retractions, negative results, and discipline-specific practice. Include privacy-preserving and restricted-data research. Examine documentation of AI-assisted research.

## Questions to investigate

- Which practices and standards are established in different disciplines?
- What information is needed to inspect or reproduce a result?
- Where are the common failure points?
- How should uncertainty, corrections, and negative results be preserved?
- When should data remain restricted?

## Expected outputs

- Tools and standards map
- provenance and research-record requirements
- practical reproducibility checklist or workflow
- integrity risks and unresolved questions.
