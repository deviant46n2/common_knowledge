## Central question

How can knowledge be represented, connected, attributed, maintained, discovered, and preserved as a durable shared resource?

## Scope

Map repositories, libraries, archives, knowledge bases, scholarly indexes, datasets, knowledge graphs, ontologies, metadata, identifiers, citation, provenance, versioning, preservation, search, multilingual access, education, licensing, and community-controlled knowledge.

## Questions to investigate

- How can records link across disciplines and formats?
- How are versions, disagreements, and corrections represented?
- What is required for long-term preservation?
- Which rights or safety considerations limit openness?
- How can people move from primary evidence to accessible explanations?

## Expected outputs

- Repository and standards map
- interoperability analysis
- traceable knowledge-record model
- preservation requirements
- rights-aware openness analysis.
