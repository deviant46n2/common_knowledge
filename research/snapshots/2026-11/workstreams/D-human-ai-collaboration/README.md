## Central question

How can people and AI systems contribute to research in useful, accountable, inspectable, and appropriately bounded ways?

## Scope

Investigate AI support for discovery, synthesis, hypothesis generation, coding, analysis, simulation, and writing; multi-agent coordination and memory; human oversight and attribution; model evaluation; open and local models; privacy, security, bias, automation failure, and fabricated evidence.

## Questions to investigate

- How can AI contributions be evaluated?
- What must be disclosed and recorded?
- Which tasks benefit from multiple models or agents?
- How are responsibility and human review preserved?
- Does AI broaden access or reinforce concentration and inequality?

## Expected outputs

- Landscape of tools and approaches
- evaluation framework
- transparent workflow patterns
- bounded experiments with explicit baselines, measures, and failure analysis.
