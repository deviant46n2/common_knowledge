## Central question

How do people and institutions discover, organize, conduct, review, publish, share, and reuse research collaboratively?

## Scope

Map platforms, institutions, communities, and practices across academic, independent, public, and community research. Examine the full research lifecycle; citizen and community science; peer review and preregistration; publication and reuse; participation barriers; and how different research settings connect.

## Questions to investigate

- What tools support each stage of the research lifecycle?
- Who can participate in practice, and who is excluded?
- Which collaboration models have evidence of sustained use?
- Where do researchers experience friction?
- When is integration or institutional support more useful than another platform?

## Expected outputs

- Field overview
- landscape directory
- selected project and organization profiles
- research-lifecycle comparison
- evidence-backed needs and opportunities.
