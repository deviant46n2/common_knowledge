## Central question

What institutional, legal, economic, and governance arrangements can sustain a shared knowledge commons while resisting capture and protecting contributors and the public interest?

## Scope

Compare nonprofits, cooperatives, public institutions, foundations, federations, commons trusts, and hybrids. Examine asset stewardship, decision-making, accountability, dispute resolution, succession, funding, fair compensation, volunteer labor, conflicts, donor/state/corporate/founder capture, political independence, contributor rights, licensing, liability, and jurisdiction.

## Questions to investigate

- Who owns and controls shared assets?
- How are decisions challenged?
- What prevents funding from buying influence?
- How are contributors treated and compensated?
- How is freedom of inquiry protected?
- What structures have survived leadership or funding changes?

## Expected outputs

- Comparative institutional models
- governance safeguards and failure modes
- funding and compensation analysis
- legal questions for qualified advice
- options and trade-offs without premature selection.
